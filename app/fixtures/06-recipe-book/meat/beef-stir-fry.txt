Beef Stir Fry with Vegetables is a quick and colorful meal inspired by Asian-style cooking. Thin slices of beef are cooked over high heat until tender and juicy, then mixed with crunchy vegetables like bell peppers, onions, broccoli, and carrots. A savory sauce made with soy sauce, garlic, ginger, and sesame oil coats every ingredient and gives the dish a rich and slightly sweet flavor. This recipe is perfect for busy days because it can be prepared in less than thirty minutes. It is healthy, high in protein, and full of fresh vegetables that add texture and color to the plate. The combination of tender beef and crisp vegetables creates a satisfying contrast in every bite. It can be served with rice or noodles for a complete meal. Many people enjoy this recipe because it is easy to adapt with different vegetables or spices depending on personal taste.

Ingredients:
- Beef strips
- Bell peppers
- Onion
- Broccoli
- Carrots
- Soy sauce
- Garlic
- Ginger
- Sesame oil
