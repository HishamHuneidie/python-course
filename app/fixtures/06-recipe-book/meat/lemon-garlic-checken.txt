A comforting and flavorful dish made with juicy chicken pieces roasted together with crispy potatoes. The chicken is marinated with fresh lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, and herbs, creating a balance between citrus freshness and savory richness. As everything cooks in the oven, the potatoes absorb the juices from the chicken and become golden and tender inside. This recipe is easy to prepare and works perfectly for lunch or dinner with family and friends. It is a complete meal that does not require many side dishes because the potatoes already make it filling. The aroma of garlic and herbs while baking makes the kitchen smell amazing. You can also customize the recipe by adding carrots, onions, or rosemary for extra flavor. It pairs well with a light salad or steamed vegetables and is ideal for anyone looking for a simple but satisfying homemade meal.

Ingredients:
- Chicken thighs or drumsticks
- Potatoes
- Garlic
- Lemon juice
- Olive oil
- Salt
- Black pepper
- Rosemary or thyme
