Classic Carbonara Pasta is a famous Italian recipe known for its creamy texture and rich flavor. Traditional carbonara is made with spaghetti, eggs, parmesan or pecorino cheese, black pepper, and crispy pancetta or bacon. The creamy sauce is created naturally by mixing eggs and cheese with the hot pasta, without using cream. This gives the dish a smooth and velvety texture that coats every strand of pasta perfectly. The salty flavor of the pancetta combines beautifully with the sharp cheese and pepper. Carbonara is simple but requires attention to timing and temperature to achieve the perfect consistency. It is a comforting meal that can be prepared quickly and enjoyed for lunch or dinner. Despite using only a few ingredients, the final result feels rich and luxurious. Many people love carbonara because it is authentic, flavorful, and one of the most iconic pasta dishes in Italian cuisine.

Ingredients:
- Spaghetti
- Eggs
- Parmesan or pecorino cheese
- Pancetta or bacon
- Black pepper
- Salt
