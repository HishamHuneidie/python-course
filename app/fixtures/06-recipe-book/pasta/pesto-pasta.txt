Pesto Pasta is a bright and aromatic Italian dish made with pasta mixed with fresh basil pesto sauce. The pesto is prepared by blending basil leaves, garlic, olive oil, parmesan cheese, and nuts such as pine nuts or walnuts. The result is a smooth green sauce with a fresh and slightly nutty flavor. When mixed with hot pasta, the sauce coats every piece and creates a simple but delicious meal. Pesto Pasta is light, quick to prepare, and perfect for lunch or dinner. It can be served warm or cold and pairs well with grilled chicken, shrimp, or vegetables. The fresh basil gives the dish a natural and refreshing aroma that makes it especially popular during warmer seasons. This recipe is loved because it uses simple ingredients while delivering bold flavor and vibrant color. It is also very versatile and easy to adapt to personal preferences.

Ingredients:
- Pasta
- Fresh basil
- Garlic
- Olive oil
- Parmesan cheese
- Pine nuts or walnuts
- Salt
- Black pepper
