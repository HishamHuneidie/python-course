Easy Tiramisu is a classic Italian dessert famous for its creamy texture and coffee flavor. It is made by layering coffee-soaked ladyfinger biscuits with a smooth mascarpone cream mixture, then finishing with cocoa powder on top. The combination of coffee, cream, and cocoa creates a balanced dessert that feels light but rich at the same time. Tiramisu is usually served cold after resting in the refrigerator for several hours, allowing the flavors to blend perfectly. This dessert is elegant yet simple to prepare, making it ideal for dinners, celebrations, or family gatherings. The mascarpone cheese gives the cream a soft and luxurious consistency, while the coffee adds depth and aroma. Some variations include chocolate shavings or a small amount of liqueur for extra flavor. Easy Tiramisu is loved because it tastes sophisticated without requiring baking, making it one of the most accessible and enjoyable Italian desserts.

Ingredients:
- Ladyfinger biscuits
- Coffee or espresso
- Mascarpone cheese
- Eggs
- Sugar
- Cocoa powder
- Vanilla extract
