Chicken Caesar Salad is a classic and satisfying dish that combines crisp romaine lettuce with grilled chicken, crunchy croutons, parmesan cheese, and creamy Caesar dressing. The grilled chicken adds protein and makes the salad more filling, while the dressing gives it a rich and savory flavor with hints of garlic and cheese. Every bite combines freshness, crunchiness, and creaminess, making it one of the most popular salads in the world. It is often served as a light lunch or dinner, but it can also work as a side dish for larger meals. The salad is easy to prepare and can be customized with bacon, boiled eggs, or cherry tomatoes. Fresh parmesan cheese on top adds extra flavor and texture. Chicken Caesar Salad is ideal for people who want a balanced meal that feels both healthy and delicious without requiring complicated cooking techniques.

Ingredients:
- Romaine lettuce
- Chicken breast
- Croutons
- Parmesan cheese
- Caesar dressing
- Garlic
- Olive oil
- Black pepper
