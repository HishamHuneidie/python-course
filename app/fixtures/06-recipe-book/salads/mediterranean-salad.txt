Mediterranean Salad is a fresh and healthy dish inspired by the traditional flavors of countries near the Mediterranean Sea. It combines crisp vegetables such as tomatoes, cucumbers, lettuce, and onions with olives and creamy feta cheese. The salad is usually dressed with olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, and a little salt, creating a light but delicious flavor. This recipe is refreshing and perfect for warm days, barbecues, or as a side dish with grilled meat or fish. The ingredients are simple, colorful, and rich in nutrients, making the salad both tasty and nutritious. The olives and feta cheese give it a salty and savory touch that balances well with the freshness of the vegetables. Mediterranean Salad is very easy to prepare and can be customized with ingredients like chickpeas, avocado, or peppers. It is an excellent option for people looking for a healthy, quick, and satisfying meal.

Ingredients:
- Tomatoes
- Cucumbers
- Lettuce
- Red onion
- Black olives
- Feta cheese
- Olive oil
- Lemon juice
- Oregano
